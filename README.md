SECTION 4 — H2: The High-Salary AI Skill Stack — What Employers Actually Pay a Premium For in 2026

Seven layers. For each: topics, why it matters to compensation, the salary signal it sends in interviews, and what most Indian courses skip. This doubles as the reader's audit checklist for any syllabus.

Layer 1 — Foundations (Python for AI, NumPy, pandas, SQL, Git/GitHub, linear algebra and calculus intuition, probability, statistics). Salary signal: none on its own — but every higher layer collapses without it, and its absence is what caps career switchers at analyst bands.

Layer 2 — Core Machine Learning with evaluation rigour (supervised/unsupervised learning, ensembles, XGBoost, feature engineering, cross-validation, bias-variance, metric selection, imbalanced data). Salary signal: "why this metric, not accuracy?" is asked in nearly every ML interview at every band; most production AI in Indian companies is still classical ML.

Layer 3 — Deep Learning (backpropagation, optimisers, CNNs, RNNs/LSTMs, transformers and attention, transfer learning, PyTorch, GPU training). Salary signal: separates candidates who can train something real from those who describe it; prerequisite for understanding LLMs.

Layer 4 — Applied Domains (NLP, computer vision, time series, recommenders). Salary signal: matches the wording of job descriptions; domain specialisation stacks with prior experience for a premium.

Layer 5 — Generative AI, LLMs & Agents (LLM fundamentals, advanced prompting, LLM APIs, open-weight models, vector databases, production RAG with chunking/hybrid search/re-ranking/evaluation, fine-tuning with SFT/LoRA/QLoRA/DPO, agents, multi-agent orchestration, LangGraph/CrewAI/AutoGen/Agents SDK, MCP and tool integration, multi-modal, guardrails). Salary signal: the 2026 premium layer — but only the production half (RAG design, fine-tuning decisions, agent reliability, evaluation). Prompting and one API call are now baseline, priced at zero premium.

Layer 6 — Production: MLOps & LLMOps (packaging, FastAPI, Docker, CI/CD, MLflow/W&B, registries, monitoring and drift, cloud deployment, LLM observability, prompt versioning, cost and latency optimisation). Salary signal: the single largest gap between "trained a model" and "engineer band"; "how would you serve this to 10,000 users?" is the question that moves candidates from the bottom of the band to the top.

Layer 7 — Professional (portfolio construction, GitHub hygiene, AI system design, case interviews, technical communication, responsible AI and governance, negotiation). Salary signal: capability you can't demonstrate and defend doesn't get priced; negotiation alone routinely changes the final number.
